## 1. Ambient-pressure superconductivity in bulk single crystals of La${}\_5$Ni${}\_4$O${}\_{10}$

- **提交日期**：2026-11-09
- **作者**：R. Zhang, P. Reiss, A. Krajewska, M. Isobe, H. Takagi, P. Puphal
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12788v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12788v1)

### 摘要
Superconductivity in the Ruddlesden-Popper (RP) nickelates has so far been confined either to multilayer compounds under high pressure or to square-planar infinite-layer-type films stabilized by epitaxial strain. Here we report the high-pressure high-temperature (HPHT) growth of bulk single crystals of the quadruple-layer RP nickelate La${}\_5$Ni${}\_4$O${}\_{13}$ ($n=4$) and reproduce the quintuple-layer La${}\_6$Ni${}\_5$O${}\_{16}$ ($n=5$) synthesis, two members of the series that had previously been accessible only as thin films. Topotactic reduction of La${}\_5$Ni${}\_4$O${}\_{13}$ single crystals with CaH${}\_2$ yields the square-planar phase La${}\_5$Ni${}\_4$O${}\_{10}$, which we show to be a bulk, ambient-pressure superconductor with $T\_\mathrm{c}^\mathrm{onset} \approx 18\,\mathrm{K}$, a shielding fraction above 60\%, and a specific-heat jump that confirms a bulk pairing transition. All crystals show besides superconductivity a coexistence with a density-wave or metal-insulator transition. The upper critical field shows a small anisotropy, $γ\approx 2.4$, which remains finite as $T \to T\_c$, indicating three-dimensional superconductivity with substantial coupling between the four-layer blocks. These results identify the square-planar nickelates $R\_{n+1}$Ni${}\_n$O${}\_{2n+2}$ as a bulk platform in which the layer number alone tunes the nickel valence into the superconducting regime, without chemical substitution, pressure, or epitaxial constraint.

## 2. Mimicry of $\textit{d}$-wave Andreev Signatures by Interlayer Pairing

- **提交日期**：2026-11-06
- **作者**：Zhongyi Zhang, Shengshan Qin, Chui-Zhen Chen, Hoi Chun Po, Jiangping Hu, Xianxin Wu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10130v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10130v1)

### 摘要
The recent discovery of high-$T\_{\rm c}$ superconductivity in bilayer nickelates has attracted enormous attention. Despite dominant theoretical studies pointing to interlayer pairing, the pairing symmetry is strongly debated due to conflicting evidence on nodeless bulk gaps and $d$-wave-like transport signatures. Here, we demonstrate that a nodeless interlayer pairing can naturally mimic $d$-wave Andreev signatures when the point contact is not evenly coupled to the two layers. The phenomenon can be understood by recognizing how a contact potential, which is odd under the layer-exchanging mirror symmetry, acts like an effective Zeeman field on the layer pseudospin. This pseudo field then binds in-gap Andreev states that sweep through the gap with the width and strength of the barrier. We further demonstrate in a realistic model for La${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_7$ that the simulated subgap conductance for a top-layer barrier can reproduce the orientation dependent ZBCP usually interpreted as a fingerprint of $\textit{d}$-wave pairing. By revealing this mirror-selective Andreev response from an interlayer pairing state, our work offers a natural scenario to reconcile the conflicting experimental evidence in bilayer nickelates and advances our understanding of unconventional superconductivity.

## 3. Direct imaging of the Ni $e\_g$ hole orbitals in the bilayer nickelate La${}\_2$SmNi${}\_2$O${}\_7$

- **提交日期**：2026-10-31
- **作者**：Ethan Saïman, Feiyu Li, Junjie Zhang, Jean-Pascal Rueff, Victor Balédent
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01118v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01118v1)

### 摘要
Models of unconventional superconductivity in cuprates and nickelates depend sensitively on the distribution of valence electrons among the $d$ orbitals. But accurately determining the orbital electron or hole occupations in these systems remains a difficult and unresolved task. Here, using $s$-core-level non-resonant inelastic x-ray scattering at the Ni $M\_1$ edge, we determine the angular distribution of the Ni $3d$ holes in a single crystal of La${}\_2$SmNi${}\_2$O${}\_7$. Our results demonstrate that the hole occupation ratio $\hz/\hx = 0.94\pm0.06$, thus significantly above the expected value of $2/3$ for a filled $d\_{z^2}$ orbitals models and close to value expected in a Hund's-coupling picture. Our results support superconductivity scenarios in which both $e\_g$ orbitals contribute to the electron-pairing mechanism in La${}\_2$SmNi${}\_2$O${}\_7$.

## 4. Separating the Density Wave Phase from Zero-Resistance Superconductivity in La${}\_4$Ni${}\_3$O${}\_{10}$

- **提交日期**：2026-10-28
- **作者**：P. Puphal, A. Suthar, B. Wehinger, V. Sundaramurthy, T. Priessnitz, M. Knauft, P. Sosa-Lizama, Y. -M. Wu, M. Hepting, J. Nuss, M. Isobe, P. A. van Aken, Y. E. Suyolcu, B. Keimer, H. Takagi, P. Reiss
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38959v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38959v1)

### 摘要
Identifying the key ingredients for superconductivity under pressure in the Ruddlesden-Popper nickelates remains a formidable challenge, due to a complex interplay between structural variations, oxygen content and location, electronically ordered phases, and the need for high pressures to reach the superconducting phase. To disentangle these complex inter-dependencies, here we study a series of clean single crystals of trilayer La${}\_4$Ni${}\_3$O${}\_{10}$. We discover that enhanced biaxial, compressive pressures induce a zero-resistance state at a 60\% lower pressure compared to previous reports. At the same time, we completely separate the low-pressure density wave phase from superconductivity, effectively ruling out that soft, quantum critical fluctuations of the density wave order parameter act as the pairing glue in this system. Our results establish biaxial, in-plane pressures as a powerful tool for promoting superconductivity in nickelates, unifying the required pressure scales for superconductivity in different Ruddlesden-Popper structures, and providing a connection to the recently discovered thin film samples.

## 5. The fate of $s\_{\pm}$ superconductivity in the bilayer nickelate La${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_7$

- **提交日期**：2026-10-26
- **作者**：Kaustubh Roy, Piers Coleman
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35814v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35814v1)

### 摘要
Motivated by the recent measurement of high-temperature superconductivity in the bilayer nickelate system La${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_7$ in the bulk as well as thin film samples, we seek to understand the role of the interlayer coupling in this system in dictating the pairing symmetry. Many proposals posit the existence of an $s\_\pm$ superconducting phase in close competition with the mroe conventional cuprate-like $d$-wave state in both weak- and strong-coupling theories. However, at least in the latter, the effects of Coulomb repulsion and the double occupancy constraint have been largely neglected. In this manuscript, we propose a mean-field fractionalization treatment that captures the full $SU(2)$ particle-hole symmetry of the t-J model at half-filling, and show how a systematic treatment of this new symmetry leads to an added Coulomb pseudopotential constraint - one that is absent in the usual $U(1)$ treatment. We then apply this new fractionalization scheme to a simplified bilayer t-J model of La${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_7$, showing how the Coulomb pseudopotential suppresses the existence of $s\_\pm$ symmetry in favor of $d$-wave pairing.

## 6. Superconducting weak links at substrate steps in bilayer nickelate films

- **提交日期**：2026-10-20
- **作者**：Yidi Liu, Yaoju Tarn, Lopa Bhatt, Puhua Zhang, Joonseo Song, Berit H. Goodge, David A. Muller, Harold Y. Hwang, Yijun Yu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27729v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27729v1)

### 摘要
Ambient-pressure superconductivity above 40 K has been realized in bilayer nickelate thin films on SrLaAlO${}\_4$ (001), yet resistive measurements often exhibit incomplete zero-resistance states and/or two-step superconducting transitions of unresolved origin. We show that these features arise from superconducting weak links at substrate step-terrace structures. A direct correlation between the azimuthal-angle-dependent two-step transition and step-terrace orientation establishes this connection, further supported by atomic-scale visualization of structurally disrupted regions at step terraces. Modeling the films as pristine (weak-link) regions with higher (lower) transition temperature reproduces essential features of the two-step transition using both a simple model and finite-element simulations. Transport measurements further reveal a strong dependence of transition characteristics - and even the occurrence of superconductivity itself - on average terrace width. These results identify step-induced weak links as the microscopic origin of two-step transitions in bilayer nickelate films, providing a framework for understanding anisotropic and percolative transport, and a route to superconducting devices.

## 7. $d$-wave superconductivity in quintuple-layer bulk superconductor La${}\_6$Ni${}\_5$O${}\_{12}$

- **提交日期**：2026-10-15
- **作者**：Zhan Wang, Yuxin Wang, Hui-Ke Jin, Kun Jiang, Jiangping Hu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22212v2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22212v2)

### 摘要
The recent realization of bulk superconductivity in the quintuple-layer nickelate La${}\_6$Ni${}\_5$O${}\_{12}$ opens new opportunities to probe the pairing symmetry of the reduced Ruddlesden--Popper (RP) nickelate family. Using an effective five-layer Hubbard model and variational Monte Carlo calculations, we find that a spin-singlet $d\_{x^2-y^2}$-wave state is energetically favored, while extended $s$-wave pairing is strongly suppressed. Based on the optimized superconducting state, we further calculate several experimentally accessible observables. The tunneling density of states exhibits a characteristic V-shaped low-energy spectrum, the superfluid density shows a linear low-temperature depletion, the Knight shift decreases below $T\_c$, and the spin-lattice relaxation rate follows $1/T\_1T\propto T^2$, together providing consistent signatures of a clean nodal superconductor. Extending the same framework to related reduced RP nickelates, we find a robust $d$-wave tendency in infinite-layer LaNiO${}\_{2}$, while trilayer La${}\_4$Ni${}\_3$O${}\_{8}$ at its nominal filling lies close to the boundary between $d$-wave superconductivity and a non-superconducting state. These results establish bulk La${}\_6$Ni${}\_5$O${}\_{12}$ as a promising platform for determining the pairing symmetry of nickelate superconductors and suggest a common $d\_{x^2-y^2}$-wave pairing tendency across the reduced RP nickelate family.

## 8. Bulk superconductivity in quintuple-layer square-planar nickelate La6Ni5O12

- **提交日期**：2026-10-14
- **作者**：Mengzhu Shi, Mei Du, Hongyu Li, Zhiwei Wang, Zhengjie Wang, Ziji Xiang, Tao Wu, Xianhui Chen
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20249v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20249v1)

### 摘要
Nickelate superconductors, sharing a similar electronic environment with cuprate superconductors, offer a promising platform for unraveling the mechanism of high-temperature superconductivity. However, the realization of superconductivity in these systems has so far been restricted to thin films or bulk samples under high-pressure. This limits the applicability of various experimental probes to study the mechanism of superconductivity, and hinders the potential application of the nickelate superconductors. Exploring bulk superconductivity at ambient pressure is therefore essential to understand its origin and enable broader experimental research. Here, we report the realization of bulk superconductivity at ambient-pressure in quintuple-layer square-planar (SP) nickelate La6Ni5O12. The SP phase is obtained by performing topotactic reaction on the quintuple-layer Ruddlesden-Popper (RP) compound La6Ni5O16. Optimized reduction condition endows La6Ni5O12 with a superconducting state that exhibits onset transition temperature of ~ 18 K, as verified by both zero resistance and diamagnetic shielding effect. Pronounced hysteresis loops revealed by magnetization and torque magnetometry demonstrate the occurrence of vortex pinning. Specific heat measurements on a polycrystalline pellet of La6Ni5O12 reveal a clear superconducting transition at ~18 K and a superconducting volume fraction of ~ 25%, which strongly supports the presence of bulk superconductivity. Our results establish an unprecedented bulk sample platform for investigating superconductivity in nickelates at ambient pressure, which paves the way for further experimental studies into the superconducting mechanism.
