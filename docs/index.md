## 1. Signatures of multiorbital density waves in the electronic Raman response of bilayer nickelates

- **提交日期**：2026-11-25
- **作者**：Lauro B. Braz, Ge He, Luis G. G. V. Dias da Silva, Ilya M. Eremin
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34471v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34471v1)

### 摘要
Recent Raman scattering experiments on bilayer nickelates have revealed pronounced spectral features in the spin-density-wave state, whose microscopic origin remains controversial, with interpretations ranging from two-magnon excitations to electronic transitions across SDW-induced gaps. Here, we demonstrate that the multiorbital electronic structure of low-pressure bilayer nickelates naturally generates distinct orbital-selective Raman signatures in different symmetry channels. Using a two-orbital bilayer model incorporating the $d\_{3z^2-r^2}$ and $d\_{x^2-y^2}$ orbitals in the orthorhombic structure, we analyze the electronic Raman response of the multiorbital spin-spinless double stripe SDW state and show that the $B\_{2g}$ response exhibits a peak at the energy scale $(M\_{z^2}+M\_{x^2})/2$, associated with SDW gap openings in weakly hybridized bands. In contrast, the dominant peak in the $B\_{1g}$ channel emerges at the multiorbital energy scale $(M\_{z^2}^2+M\_{x^2}^2)^{1/2}$, reflecting the strongly hybridized character of the relevant electronic states. These distinct energy scales provide a unified interpretation of several Raman signatures observed in La${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_7$ and offer a means of disentangling the orbital contributions to the SDW order parameters. Our results highlight that in contrast to the superconducting state, Raman peak positions in the SDW state cannot generally be identified with a single SDW gap and establish symmetry-resolved electronic Raman scattering as a sensitive probe of multiorbital density-wave order.

## 2. Valence band, electron correlation, and possible temperature dependent change in the electronic structure near $E\_F$ of a candidate of ambient-pressure nickelate superconductors Sr${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_5$Cl${}\_2$

- **提交日期**：2026-11-23
- **作者**：Ryota Goban, Daiki Ootsuki, Kenichi Ozawa, Akira Yasui, Masayuki Ochi, Kazuhiko Kuroki, Ryo Matsumoto, Kensei Terashima, Yoshihiko Takano, Yuji Muraoka, Takayoshi Yokoya
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30088v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30088v1)

### 摘要
Photoemission spectroscopy (PES) studies of Sr${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_5$Cl${}\_2$ (Sr3252), a candidate ambient-pressure bilayer nickelate superconductor, have been performed to investigate the electronic structure. Valence band spectra, which are compared with DFT and DFT+U calculations, reveal dominant Ni 3$d$ $t\_{2g}$-derived states located around 0.6 eV binding energy with a shoulder around 0.2 eV that can be attributed to states with a dominant Ni 3$d\_{3z2-r2}$ character. The overall valence band shape is better reproduced by the simulated spectrum of DFT+U (= 3 eV), providing an experimental estimation of the strength of electron correlation. High-resolution PES near the Fermi level ($E\_F$) exhibits marked temperature dependence, which cannot be fully understood by a broadening due to coupling of a photo-hole to phonons alone and suggests change in electronic structure. The results provide fundamental electronic structure information of Sr3252, which is useful to further explore the superconductivity of Sr3252.

## 3. Minimal Three-Band Model and $d$-Wave Superconductivity in La${}\_4$Ni${}\_3$O${}\_8$

- **提交日期**：2026-11-21
- **作者**：Ao Zhang, Chao Deng, Wenfeng Wu, Run Yang, Zhixiang Shi, Liang Si
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28747v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28747v1)

### 摘要
Motivated by the recent observation of ambient-pressure superconductivity in Pr- and Y-substituted La${}\_4$Ni${}\_3$O${}\_8$, we combined density-functional theory (DFT), dynamical mean-field theory (DMFT), random phase approximation (RPA), and fluctuation exchange approximation (FLEX) methods to study its electronic structure and superconducting pairing symmetry. DFT band analysis indicates that the low-energy states are mainly dominated by three Ni-3$d\_{x^2-y^2}$ orbitals with weak $d$-$p$ hybridization. Multi-orbital DMFT calculations yield an average $d\_{x^2-y^2}$ filling of about 0.87, close to the optimal value for infinite-layer nickelates. By comparing the DMFT results of $d\_{x^2-y^2}$ only and full Ni-3$d$ models, we find that their electronic properties are almost completely consistent, demonstrating the effectiveness of the simplified $d\_{x^2-y^2}$ model. RPA and FLEX calculations based on this model consistently indicate a dominant $d\_{x^2-y^2}$-wave pairing instability. These results establish La${}\_4$Ni${}\_3$O${}\_8$ as a system whose electronic structure closely resembles that of hole-doped cuprates, suggesting that the electronic pockets near the $Γ$- and $A$-points, which are characteristic of infinite-layer nickelates, may not be essential for superconductivity. The calculated superconducting transition temperature ($T\_C$) is approximately 10\,K, in good agreement with experimental observations.

## 4. Reconciling Models for Infinite-Layer Nickelate Superconductors: Role of Interstitial-$s$ Band or $A$-Pocket

- **提交日期**：2026-11-18
- **作者**：Ao Zhang, Chao Deng, Leonard M. Verhoff, Mi Jiang, Run Yang, Zhixiang Shi, Karsten Held, Liang Si
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24288v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24288v1)

### 摘要
Understanding the minimal model that captures the essential physics of infinite-layer nickelate superconductors remains a central challenge. Using first-principles density-functional theory combined with dynamical mean-field theory (DFT+DMFT), we compute and compare the strongly correlated electronic structures and optical spectra of various effective models for the infinite-layer nickelate LaNiO${}\_2$, ranging from a $d\_{x^2-y^2}$ 1-band model to a full 6-band model with all Ni-$3d$ plus an interstitial-$s$ orbital. The results demonstrate that Ni-$3d$ 5-band models overestimate the Hundness picture, whereas 6-band models, which explicitly include the interstitial-$s$ band and have an $A$-pocket, recover the characteristic Mottness physics. Importantly, the influence of the interstitial-$s$ band on the correlations of the $d\_{x^2-y^2}$ band is negligible. Thus, superconductivity in infinite-layer nickelates can be reliably described within a single-band Hubbard framework, while the accurate description of inter-orbital properties such as (optical) conductivities requires the inclusion of at least the interstitial-$s$ band.

## 5. Layer-dependent $s^{\pm}$ superconducting pairing and influence of incipient bands in La${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_7$ thin films on real LaSrAlO${}\_4$ substrate

- **提交日期**：2026-11-17
- **作者**：Yang Zhang, Philip M. Dee, Ling-Fang Lin, Adriana Moreo, Thomas A. Maier, Elbio Dagotto
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23595v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23595v1)

### 摘要
Motivated by recent advances in ambient-pressure superconducting La${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_7$ (LNO) thin films, we theoretically investigate these films by explicitly incorporating the realistic LaSrAlO${}\_4$ (LSAO) substrate. The Fermi-surface topology of the nickelate thin film closely resembles that of the high-pressure bulk phase, with similar $α$ and $β$ sheets and a small hole-like $γ$ pocket originating from the surface bilayer (BL3). The bonding $d\_{3z^2-r^2}$ state of the interfacial bilayer (BL1, adjacent to the LSAO substrate) lies only $\sim 5$ meV below the Fermi level, giving rise to an incipient $γ$ band, while for the middle bilayer (BL2) its energy is much lower ($\sim 95$ meV). Remarkably, no significant spontaneous charge transfer from the LSAO substrate to the LNO film is found. Instead, \{\it symmetry-allowed charge disproportionation\} occurs between the BLs, with hole doping in BL3 and electron doping in BL1 and BL2. Via the weak-coupling Random Phase Approximation, we find a strong $s^{\pm}$-wave superconducting pairing instability in BL3, while $d\_{x^2-y^2}$ and $s^{\pm}$ are nearly degenerate in the other bilayers BL1 and BL2. Including the incipient $γ$ band via the Dynamical Cluster Approximation beyond weak coupling, robust $s^{\pm}$-wave pairing is observed in all three layers

## 6. Failure of Mean-Field Critical Scaling and Two Distinct Dissipation Regimes in La${}\_2$SmNi${}\_2$O${}\_{7-δ}$

- **提交日期**：2026-11-16
- **作者**：Yongze Ye, Feiyu Li, Di Peng, Junjie Zhang, Hai-Hu Wen
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21536v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21536v1)

### 摘要
Recently, superconductivity with an onset transition temperature ($T\_{\mathrm{c}}^{\mathrm{onset}}$) up to 96 K has been observed in the $\mathrm{La}\_{2-x}\mathrm{Sm}\_{1+x}\mathrm{Ni}\_2\mathrm{O}\_{7-δ}$ system under pressure, providing an attractive platform for investigating superconducting mechanisms in nickelates. Here, we systematically analyzed the critical scaling and vortex dynamics for the $\mathrm{La}\_2\mathrm{Sm}\mathrm{Ni}\_2\mathrm{O}\_{7-δ}$ single crystal with $T\_{\mathrm{c}}^{\mathrm{onset}}=92.5$ K under pressure. Within the Ullah--Dorsey framework, neither the two-dimensional nor three-dimensional scaling provides a good collapse near $T\_{\mathrm{c}}^{\mathrm{MF}}$. This failure may be associated with the multiband composite order parameter of bilayer nickelates, in which pairing is mainly driven by antiferromagnetic exchange in the $d\_{z^2}$ orbital, whereas the superfluid stiffness is mainly governed by the $d\_{x^2-y^2}$ orbital. The two-step decrease in the logarithmic resistivity reveals two distinct dissipative regimes. The low-temperature regime is well described by the thermally assisted flux flow (TAFF) model, whereas the high-temperature regime cannot be explained by the vortex dynamics involved with the superconducting fluctuation. The crossover between the two regimes may be associated with the orbital-dependent evolution of vortex cores. These results suggest the significant role of the composite order parameter in critical fluctuations and vortex-motion dissipation in nickelates.

## 7. Theoretical exploration of ambient-pressure superconductivity in the bilayer nickelate: A strategy of atomic substitution for tuning chemical pressure

- **提交日期**：2026-11-13
- **作者**：Tomo-Hiko Hamada, Kensei Ushio, Shu Kamiyama, Kazuhiko Kuroki, Hirofumi Sakakibara
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17709v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17709v1)

### 摘要
The possibility of ambient-pressure superconductivity in $\mathrm{La\_3Ni\_2O\_7}$ is theoretically explored via chemical substitution. Using first-principles and many-body calculations, we propose substituting $\mathrm{Ba}$ at the lanthanide site to exert chemical pressure, thereby mitigating the octahedral distortions that suppress superconductivity. Phonon calculations demonstrate the dynamical stability of the high-symmetry tetragonal phase in $\mathrm{La\_{3-\it{x}}Ba\_{\it{x}}Ni\_2O\_7}$ at a small doping ratio of $x \sim 0.15$. By applying the fluctuation exchange approximation to an effective model Hamiltonian derived from this stable structure, we calculate the Eliashberg eigenvalue $λ$ to evaluate the relative superconducting transition temperature $T\_\mathrm{c}$ to existing unconventional superconductors. We conclude that $s\_\pm$-wave superconductivity is likely to emerge, as the required $\mathrm{Ba}$ concentration ($x \sim 0.15$) is sufficiently small to minimize the detrimental suppression of superconductivity induced by hole doping.

## 8. Ambient-pressure superconductivity in bulk single crystals of La${}\_5$Ni${}\_4$O${}\_{10}$

- **提交日期**：2026-11-09
- **作者**：R. Zhang, P. Reiss, A. Krajewska, M. Isobe, H. Takagi, P. Puphal
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12788v2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12788v2)

### 摘要
Superconductivity in the Ruddlesden--Popper (RP) nickelates has so far been confined either to multilayer compounds under high pressure or to square-planar infinite-layer-type films stabilized by epitaxial strain. Here, we report the high-pressure high-temperature (HPHT) growth of bulk single crystals of the quadruple-layer RP nickelate La${}\_5$Ni${}\_4$O${}\_{13}$ ($n=4$) and reproduce the quintuple-layer La${}\_6$Ni${}\_5$O${}\_{16}$ ($n=5$) synthesis, phases previously accessible only as thin films. Topotactic reduction of La${}\_5$Ni${}\_4$O${}\_{13}$ single crystals with CaH${}\_2$ yields the square-planar La${}\_5$Ni${}\_4$O${}\_{10}$, which we show to be a bulk, ambient-pressure superconductor with $T\_\mathrm{c}^\mathrm{onset}\approx18.7$\,K, a shielding fraction above 60\%, and a specific-heat jump that confirms a bulk pairing transition. In transport, all crystals show an anomaly near 90~K reminiscent of a density-wave like transition, which may arise from a partially gapped Fermi surface or from the non-superconducting volume fraction. The upper critical field shows a small anisotropy, $γ\approx 2.4$, which remains finite as $T \to T\_c$, indicating three-dimensional superconductivity with substantial coupling between the four-layer blocks. These results identify the square-planar nickelates $R\_{n+1}$Ni${}\_n$O${}\_{2n+2}$ as a bulk platform in which the layer number alone tunes the nickel valence into the superconducting regime, without chemical substitution, pressure, or epitaxial constraint.

## 9. Mimicry of $\textit{d}$-wave Andreev Signatures by Interlayer Pairing

- **提交日期**：2026-11-06
- **作者**：Zhongyi Zhang, Shengshan Qin, Chui-Zhen Chen, Hoi Chun Po, Jiangping Hu, Xianxin Wu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10130v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10130v1)

### 摘要
The recent discovery of high-$T\_{\rm c}$ superconductivity in bilayer nickelates has attracted enormous attention. Despite dominant theoretical studies pointing to interlayer pairing, the pairing symmetry is strongly debated due to conflicting evidence on nodeless bulk gaps and $d$-wave-like transport signatures. Here, we demonstrate that a nodeless interlayer pairing can naturally mimic $d$-wave Andreev signatures when the point contact is not evenly coupled to the two layers. The phenomenon can be understood by recognizing how a contact potential, which is odd under the layer-exchanging mirror symmetry, acts like an effective Zeeman field on the layer pseudospin. This pseudo field then binds in-gap Andreev states that sweep through the gap with the width and strength of the barrier. We further demonstrate in a realistic model for La${}\_3$Ni${}\_2$O${}\_7$ that the simulated subgap conductance for a top-layer barrier can reproduce the orientation dependent ZBCP usually interpreted as a fingerprint of $\textit{d}$-wave pairing. By revealing this mirror-selective Andreev response from an interlayer pairing state, our work offers a natural scenario to reconcile the conflicting experimental evidence in bilayer nickelates and advances our understanding of unconventional superconductivity.

## 10. Direct imaging of the Ni $e\_g$ hole orbitals in the bilayer nickelate La${}\_2$SmNi${}\_2$O${}\_7$

- **提交日期**：2026-10-31
- **作者**：Ethan Saïman, Feiyu Li, Junjie Zhang, Jean-Pascal Rueff, Victor Balédent
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01118v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01118v1)

### 摘要
Models of unconventional superconductivity in cuprates and nickelates depend sensitively on the distribution of valence electrons among the $d$ orbitals. But accurately determining the orbital electron or hole occupations in these systems remains a difficult and unresolved task. Here, using $s$-core-level non-resonant inelastic x-ray scattering at the Ni $M\_1$ edge, we determine the angular distribution of the Ni $3d$ holes in a single crystal of La${}\_2$SmNi${}\_2$O${}\_7$. Our results demonstrate that the hole occupation ratio $\hz/\hx = 0.94\pm0.06$, thus significantly above the expected value of $2/3$ for a filled $d\_{z^2}$ orbitals models and close to value expected in a Hund's-coupling picture. Our results support superconductivity scenarios in which both $e\_g$ orbitals contribute to the electron-pairing mechanism in La${}\_2$SmNi${}\_2$O${}\_7$.

## 11. Separating the Density Wave Phase from Zero-Resistance Superconductivity in La${}\_4$Ni${}\_3$O${}\_{10}$

- **提交日期**：2026-10-28
- **作者**：P. Puphal, A. Suthar, B. Wehinger, V. Sundaramurthy, T. Priessnitz, M. Knauft, P. Sosa-Lizama, Y. -M. Wu, M. Hepting, J. Nuss, M. Isobe, P. A. van Aken, Y. E. Suyolcu, B. Keimer, H. Takagi, P. Reiss
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38959v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38959v1)

### 摘要
Identifying the key ingredients for superconductivity under pressure in the Ruddlesden-Popper nickelates remains a formidable challenge, due to a complex interplay between structural variations, oxygen content and location, electronically ordered phases, and the need for high pressures to reach the superconducting phase. To disentangle these complex inter-dependencies, here we study a series of clean single crystals of trilayer La${}\_4$Ni${}\_3$O${}\_{10}$. We discover that enhanced biaxial, compressive pressures induce a zero-resistance state at a 60\% lower pressure compared to previous reports. At the same time, we completely separate the low-pressure density wave phase from superconductivity, effectively ruling out that soft, quantum critical fluctuations of the density wave order parameter act as the pairing glue in this system. Our results establish biaxial, in-plane pressures as a powerful tool for promoting superconductivity in nickelates, unifying the required pressure scales for superconductivity in different Ruddlesden-Popper structures, and providing a connection to the recently discovered thin film samples.
